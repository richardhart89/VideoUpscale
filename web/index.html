What this tool can (and cannot) restore

AVI and MP4 are containers; codec support depends on your FFmpeg build. Cleanup runs before upscaling, with gentle sharpening last. AI uses individual frames, not a temporal video model.

This version targets SDR footage, one video and the first audio track. It does not preserve subtitles, chapters, extra audio tracks, HDR, or rotation. AI normalizes variable frame rate to constant frame rate and can use substantial temporary disk space.

No algorithm can recover the exact detail that was never recorded. Compare a moving scene before committing to a long export.

Made for deliberate restoration, not artificial detail.
